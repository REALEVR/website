How it works

From booking to live tour in days

  1. Book a shoot

    Call or email us with the property location and size. We confirm a date that suits you.

  2. We capture

    Our team photographs the property in 360° with professional equipment, usually in a single visit.

  3. We build

    We stitch, polish and brand the tour, adding hotspots, floor navigation and information points.

  4. You share

    Get a link you can share anywhere: listings, social media, WhatsApp or your own website.

About REALEVR

The future of real estate is virtual

Real estate keeps changing, and so do the expectations of buyers and sellers. Static photos and descriptions no longer tell the whole story. At REALEVR we let you step inside a property and get a real sense of space, layout and ambience.

Our vision is a world where anyone, anywhere, can explore a property as if they were there in person. We combine modern virtual tour technology with a solid understanding of the Ugandan property market, so the platform is practical as well as new.
